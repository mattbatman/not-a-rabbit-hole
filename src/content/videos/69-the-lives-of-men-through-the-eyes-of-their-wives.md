  <p>What’s one of the best ways a wife can make life easier for her husband?
  </p><p>
  👀👀👀
  </p><p>
  What helps more than keeping the house in order, even when the mess was his?
  </p><p>
  Cleanliness is objectively important, and something everyone should appreciate regardless of how much they show it or prioritize it themselves.
  </p><p>
  Marriage is teamwork. Sometimes you make the mess. Sometimes she does. Sometimes life gets busy and everything gets a little out of control. That’s just reality when you have kids, work, errands, and a hundred things happening at once.
  </p><p>
  A wife who sees a mess and simply handles it without keeping score is showing something valuable. She’s saying we’re in this together.
  </p><p>
  And husbands notice that.
  </p><p>
  There’s something deeply appreciated about coming home and seeing that your wife has taken care of things. The house feels calmer. Life feels a little more organized. You feel taken care of.
  </p><p>
  The same should go the other direction. A husband shouldn’t expect his wife to clean up after him while he sits around doing nothing. If she needs help, he should get up and help. If she’s carrying a heavy load, he should carry some of it.
  </p><p>
  That’s what marriage is supposed to look like. Two people looking for ways to make each other’s lives better.
  </p><p>
  And honestly, sometimes the strongest thing you can do is just grab the broom, pick up the toys, wipe down the counter, and get it done.
  </p><p>
  No speech. No scorekeeping. Just taking care of your family.
  </p><p>
  How much better would marriage be if both husband and wife spent more time looking for ways to serve each other continually?</p>
